## Signed and notarized installers

NSM Flow is now signed with Nathaniel School of Music's Apple Developer ID and
notarized by Apple. macOS opens it like any other app — no "damaged" warning and
no right-click workaround.

## Local dictation, no account needed

Hold your shortcut, speak, release. Everything is transcribed on your own
computer. Google sign-in stays optional and is only used to show your name and
email locally — no transcript, recording, or token leaves your machine.

## Musician-grade typing

Say the chord and get the symbol: **B♭maj7**, **G7♭9**, **Dø7**, **A/C♯**,
**6/8**, **♩ = 120**. Spoken accidentals become real Unicode notation instead of
"B flat".

## India-aware spelling

A conservative built-in lexicon covers Indian names, cities, food, clothing,
deities, temples, schools, and colleges — without turning ordinary English words
into false matches.

## Writing modes that follow the app

Email, WhatsApp, AI prompts, documents, code, and music software each get their
own formatting rules automatically.
