# NSM Flow 0.3.0-alpha.8

## New Mac dictation key: `~`

Hold the key left of 1 to talk. Double-tap it for hands-free and tap once more
to finish. A quick tap still types the ` character. Shift+Escape throws a
recording away. Change either under General › Transcribe Shortcut.

## Smarter writing

- "On the twelfth, sorry, the fourteenth" becomes "on the fourteenth". "Scratch
  that" removes what came before.
- Lists of three or more items become bullets or numbered steps.
- Set an email sign-off under Post Process and it is added to every email.
- Ordinary words stay ordinary: "a seven year old" and "a sharp reply" no longer
  turn into chord names. Cm(maj7) and Gaug7 are now written correctly.

## Fixes

- AI formatting never receives your words while it is switched off.
- AI formatting gives up after 30 seconds and uses the local clean-up instead.
- A failed paste no longer loses what was on your clipboard.
- Changing a shortcut can no longer leave dictation without a key.
- Whisper Small is now the recommended first model for Indian English and
  Indian languages.

Mac installers are signed and notarized. Windows installers are unsigned and may
show a SmartScreen warning.
